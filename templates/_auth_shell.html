SL Shelf-Life Monitor

Know how long your produce really has.

A Raspberry Pi watches temperature and humidity where your fruit and vegetables are stored, and estimates how much shelf life each item has left — on the device, with no cloud round trip.

  • {{ icon('thermometer', 18) }}BMP280 and SHT31 sensing, sampled continuously
  • {{ icon('cpu', 18) }}Inference runs locally on the Raspberry Pi 5
  • {{ icon('activity', 18) }}Degradation integrated over the conditions actually experienced
  • {{ icon('bell', 18) }}Alerts before produce is wasted, not after
